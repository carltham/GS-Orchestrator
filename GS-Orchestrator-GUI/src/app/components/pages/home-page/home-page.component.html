Welcome to GS-Orchestrator

GS-Orchestrator is a lightweight, zero-configuration local service discovery & port management orchestrator. It enables multi-service microservice architectures to automatically allocate unique local ports, track service health, detect unmanaged local background daemons, and manage service lifecycles without manual port collision handling.

⚡ Dynamic Port Allocation

Automatically reserves non-overlapping base ports for backend and frontend services on startup.

🔍 Background Process Scanner

Monitors active TCP ports across the system to locate unregistered development servers & daemons.

💓 Health Reporting & Heartbeats

Accepts periodic health pings from registered clients to keep service registry states accurate.

🚀 @gs/orchestrator-client

Provides zero-setup client initialization scripts, startup handlers, and prestart hooks for projects.
